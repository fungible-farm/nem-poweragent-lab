Ring buffer — lock per consumer

The distribution primitive (docs/PSCADOSSE.md). One producer writes logical indices into a ring; each consumer holds its own cursor. A slow consumer lags; items behind the drop-oldest frontier are lost by it, but the producer and other consumers are never blocked.
